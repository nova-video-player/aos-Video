# R8 is invoked with tree-shrinking, optimization, and obfuscation all disabled.
# These rules protect JNI-bound members and keep stack traces readable.
# The -keep rules also serve as documentation: if -dontshrink is ever removed,
# they are the minimum set needed to keep the app working.

-dontshrink
-dontoptimize
-dontobfuscate
-dontrepackage

-keepattributes SourceFile,LineNumberTable,Exceptions,InnerClasses,Signature,EnclosingMethod,RuntimeVisibleAnnotations,RuntimeVisibleParameterAnnotations
