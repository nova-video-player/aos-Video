SOFA profile metadata, sources and notices
Updated 22 September 2026

Both profiles contain measured SimpleFreeFieldHRIR data. The TV filename
identifies Nova's selected use; it is not a measured TV/room correction model.

HRIR_FULL2DEG_TV_speakers.sofa
Author: Benjamin Bernschuetz, TH Koeln (Cologne University of Applied Sciences).
Dataset: HRIR_FULL2DEG MKII, Neumann KU100, 48000 Hz.
Source: https://sofacoustics.org/data/database/thk/
Reference: https://audiogroup.web.th-koeln.de/PUBLIKATIONEN/Bernschuetz_DAGA2013.pdf
Embedded license: CC 3.0 BY-SA
License: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/3.0/
SHA-256: d3671e6829323b93b7ae95aff6d9316d15d6c128b7f2ab615c21cc3ab085ddb7
The SOFA file is unchanged. Nova's derived four-path FIR coefficients in
native/avos/Source/sofa_tv_fir.h are also provided under CC BY-SA 3.0, with
attribution to the dataset author. They were generated by Nova in September
2026 for 1 m speaker spacing, a centered listener at 2 m, and ear-height
speakers. The derivation uses interpolation, relative propagation, multi-point
regularized inversion, frequency blending, windowing and gain normalization.
Source and reproducible generator: native/avos/test/design-sofa-tv.py.

University of York, AudioLab.

48000 Hz, 256 taps, 8802 positions. Publisher processing includes low-frequency
extension and diffuse-field equalization. The bundled SOFA file is unchanged.
SHA-256: 9af7cb19531e52fb7ae8ec92621e6ab62b1d5fe584b3742be36699a0ddb0ccd4
